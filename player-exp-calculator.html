Player EXP is your account level, from 1 to 60. Levels give you Talent points and unlock higher-level equipment, plus higher Hero and Familiar levels.

Monster hunting

Every hunt gives EXP. Higher-level monsters cost more energy, so EXP is roughly proportional to the energy you spend.

Fighting enemy troops

You get EXP for each enemy troop you wound or kill.

Other sources

Forging equipment

Finishing a forge gives EXP. The equipment grade decides how much, not its level or your might.

Guild and Admin quests

Rarer quests (Common up to Legendary) give more, and the reward grows as you level up.

Hero stages

Completing or sweeping a stage gives EXP, more on higher stages. Sweeping an Elite stage gives double because it costs double energy. This depends on your stage progress, not your level.

Colosseum

Each battle gives EXP, and the amount grows as you level up.

Turf quests and EXP items

Turf quests and Player EXP items also add EXP.

Tips

Levels 1 to 50 go quickly compared with the last ten levels.

Levels 52 to 55 need roughly 1M to 1.2M EXP each: slow, but manageable.

Levels 57 to 60 alone take 46.87M EXP, about 77% of the 60.76M total. Plan ahead and save your EXP sources for them.

Data: Lords Mobile Wiki, checked against IGG's official EXP calculator. Hunts and troops are rounded up. Admin quest, Guild quest, Colosseum and Hero Stage EXP aren't counted in the action totals.
